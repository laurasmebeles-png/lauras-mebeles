## 3. Mazas un stūra virtuves

## Small kitchens and corner layouts

### Small kitchens

In a small kitchen, the layout needs to make space for cooking, dishes and appliances. We plan the worktop area and cabinet positions so there’s room to move around.

### Corner kitchens

We plan corner kitchens with easy access to shelves and enough clearance for doors to open. Appliance positions are part of the layout from the start.

## Layout, materials and cost

### 01 / Layout

We plan the cooking area and overall layout to suit the dimensions of your room.

### 02 / Materials and details

Together, we choose the cabinet doors, worktop, fittings and handles to suit your ideas and budget.

### 03 / Appliances and equipment

We talk through the built-in appliances you plan to use and where they will go.

### What affects the cost of a custom kitchen?

The cabinet doors, worktop and fittings you choose all affect the price. The size and layout of your kitchen matter too. Our quote includes design, delivery and installation. We’ll agree on appliances separately.
