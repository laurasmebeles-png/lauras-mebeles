Ask us a question
Can I get in touch before I have a finished design?

Yes. Tell us what furniture you have in mind. If you have rough measurements or examples you like, mention them in your enquiry. We’ll talk through the rest together.

What affects the cost of made-to-measure furniture?

The price depends on dimensions, layout, front and worktop materials, fittings and any additional features. We design and make your furniture, deliver it and install it. Transport and installation outside Latvia are priced separately.

Do you also make wardrobes and other furniture?

Yes. As well as kitchens, we make built-in wardrobes and furniture for hallways, bathrooms, living rooms and other spaces.

Do you deliver and install furniture outside Latvia?

Yes. We have delivered and installed furniture across Europe, including Germany, the Netherlands, Sweden and the UK. We handle the full process, from design and production to delivery and installation. Transport and installation outside Latvia are priced separately. Tell us where your project is so we can discuss measuring, delivery and installation arrangements.

How long will my furniture take to make?

We’ll agree the lead time with you individually. It depends on the project, materials and our workshop schedule. Let us know when you would like your furniture to be ready.
