Before our first conversation

What we’ll plan together

01

Room dimensions

We plan the wardrobe to fit the room, taking ceiling height, door positions and space to move into account.

02

Inside your wardrobe

We’ll plan the shelves, drawers and hanging space around what you need to store.

03

Doors and finishes

Together, we’ll choose hinged or sliding doors and a finish that suits the room.

What affects the cost of a built-in wardrobe?

The price depends on the dimensions, door type, materials and internal fittings, including shelves and drawers.
