If you have questions about how we use your personal data, please email us at info@laurasmebeles.lv.

Your messages and enquiries

When you email or call us, we handle the name and contact details you provide, the contents of your message and any information you share about your furniture project. We need at least one way to contact you so we can reply.

We use these details to reply, discuss your project and prepare a quote if you ask for one. We use this information to take steps at your request before entering into a contract. For other enquiries, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in handling and responding to messages.

Our staff who handle enquiries and the email providers we rely on can access messages as needed.

We keep the information while we deal with your enquiry and for any follow-up. We may keep it longer when legally required or to defend legal claims.

Website operation and security

Vercel hosts the website online. To serve pages and prevent technical or security problems, it may process information such as your IP address, the page requested and the time of the request. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in keeping the site available and secure. The hosting settings determine how long technical logs are kept.

Preview diagnostics stay in the page’s memory until the page is reloaded or closed.

Cookies and Google Maps

We save your choice about Google Maps in this browser’s local storage. This record is necessary for the site to honour your choice, including when you switch languages. Your choice stays valid for 180 days. Once it expires, we remove the record the next time you visit.

Preference stored in your browser lauras-cookie-preferences

We load Google Maps on the contact page with your consent. When the map loads, Google receives connection data such as your IP address and may use cookies. Google explains the purposes and duration of its cookies in its policies.

You can withdraw permission in Cookie settings on any page or on the contact page itself. This removes the map. You can manage any Google cookies already stored in your browser settings.

When you follow a link to another website, that provider’s privacy policy applies there.

  • Google’s use of cookies

Data processing outside the European Economic Area

Vercel and Google may process data outside the European Economic Area. Vercel’s data processing terms provide for the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. Google explains its transfer mechanisms, including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, in its policies. Contact us for details of the applicable safeguards or copies of them.

  • Vercel data processing terms
  • Google’s rules for data transfers

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data or restrict how we use it. Where applicable, you can ask to receive or transfer your data and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent at any time. This applies to future processing.

You can email us to exercise your rights. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority.
