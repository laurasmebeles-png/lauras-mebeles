If you have questions about how we use your personal data, please email us at info@laurasmebeles.lv.

Your messages and enquiries

When you email or call us, we use the information you provide. This may include your name, contact details, your message or details about the furniture you have in mind. An email address or telephone number is enough for us to reply.

If you ask us for a quote, we use your details to discuss your project and prepare the quote. These are steps we take at your request before entering into a contract. For other enquiries, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in handling and responding to them.

Staff who handle enquiries can read your message. Email service providers may also process the information when we send or receive emails.

We keep the information while we handle your enquiry and communicate with you about it. We may keep it longer when required by law or to defend legal claims.

Website operation and security

Vercel hosts the website online. To serve pages and prevent technical or security problems, it may process information such as your IP address, the page requested and the time of the request. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in keeping the site available and secure. The hosting settings determine how long technical logs are kept.

Information used to test the preview stays in the open page’s memory. It disappears when the page is reloaded or closed.

Cookies

Cookies are small files that a website can save in your browser.

A necessary browser record lets the site remember your cookie preferences. We rely on your consent to use Google Maps, analytics and advertising tools. You can still browse the site and contact us if you decline.

We save your cookie choices in this browser’s local storage. They apply across all site languages and remain valid for 180 days. After that, we ask you to choose again. We delete the expired record when you next visit the site or when it expires while the page is open.

Preference stored in your browser lauras-cookie-preferences

We load Google Maps on the contact page with your consent. When the map loads, Google receives connection data such as your IP address and may use cookies. Google explains the purposes and duration of its cookies in its policies.

We plan to use Google Analytics 4 to understand how people use the site, and Meta Pixel and LinkedIn Insight Tag to measure how our ads perform. Google Tag Manager will load these tools according to your choices. These tools are inactive in this preview.

You can change your choices at any time by opening “Cookie settings” at the bottom of any page. You can manage any third-party cookies already stored in your browser settings.

If we add new tools or change how we use data, we will update this information and ask for your consent to the relevant changes.

When you follow a link to another website, that provider’s privacy policy applies there.

Data processing outside the European Economic Area

Vercel and Google may process data outside the European Economic Area. Vercel’s data processing terms provide for the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. Google explains its transfer mechanisms, including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, in its policies. Contact us for details of the applicable safeguards or copies of them.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data or restrict how we use it. Where applicable, you can receive and transfer your data and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before you withdrew consent.

To exercise your rights, email info@laurasmebeles.lv. You can complain to the Latvian Data State Inspectorate or the competent data protection authority in the country where you live or work.
